# Human Rights and Affected-Person Safeguards

This is an initial product-design screen, not a formal human-rights impact assessment or legal opinion.

The tool's subject is a code change, not the contributor. The product must not infer personal traits, protected characteristics, competence, intent, trustworthiness, or worth from code or repository activity. It must not rank contributors, generate character judgments, or decide access to jobs, education, housing, credit, health care, legal rights, or public services. A reviewer must inspect evidence and retain the ability to reject a finding.

Potential harms include sensitive information being repeated in reports, heuristic false positives influencing treatment of an author, security findings being over-trusted, and results being repurposed for employee monitoring or performance scoring. Mitigations are data minimization, no source excerpts or secret values, neutral finding language, visible confidence and limitations, no people metrics, no automated comments/decisions, and an explicit high-impact-use boundary.

Before deployment in a new context, consult affected stakeholders, evaluate disparate impact and accessibility, establish review/appeal and correction channels where relevant, and assess applicable human-rights and data-protection obligations. This repository has not tested real-world impacts or made a jurisdictional classification. Any proposal to score people, profile authors, or automate consequential decisions is outside the current product scope and requires a new product, legal, and human-rights assessment.
